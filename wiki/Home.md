# Signals Reforged wiki

Signals Reforged brings railway infrastructure to **vanilla minecarts**: automatic track discovery, routing to named
stations, block, chain and path signals, switch motors, signal boxes, timetables and cargo logistics.

**Current version:** 0.1.0-beta.3 (public beta) for Minecraft 26.3 with NeoForge 26.3.0.109 or newer.
Download: [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/signals-reforged) · [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/signals-reforged).
